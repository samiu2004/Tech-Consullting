How to reach us

Use the form on this page. It asks for your name, your email address, and your message. Your email address is only used to reply to you, and it is not published or shared.

Report a correction

Use the form on this page and include the page title, the statement to review, and a reliable source. Corrections are checked against the source before anything on the page changes.

Suggest a tool or a guide

Explain the use case and what readers need help with. Suggestions are read alongside the editorial standards: a tool is only recommended after it has been checked, and no suggestion guarantees a listing.

For partnerships

Future sponsorship and affiliate proposals will be evaluated separately from editorial recommendations. Paid placements must be identified clearly.

What happens next

Messages sent through the form reach the TechConsulting team, and every message is read. We do not promise a reply time, and we do not publish your email address. If your note needs a reply, include an email address where we can reach you.

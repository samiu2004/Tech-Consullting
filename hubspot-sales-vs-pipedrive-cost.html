HubSpot vs Pipedrive: choose a CRM your team will use

Compare HubSpot and Pipedrive for a small sales team: workflow fit, seat costs, upgrades and a practical pilot.

By TechConsulting · Updated 18 November 2026 · Documentation-based

THE DECISION IN BRIEF

Choose around your sales process—not a feature checklist.

HubSpot

Consider HubSpot when sales needs to work alongside a broader customer platform.

Pipedrive

Consider Pipedrive when managing deals and follow-up is the immediate priority.

The short answer

A CRM should make the next sales action obvious. If you need a wider customer platform spanning departments, investigate HubSpot and the specific hubs your business would use. If your immediate problem is deals getting lost between enquiry and follow-up, investigate Pipedrive’s sales-focused plans. This is an editorial recommendation from provider documentation, not a hands-on usability verdict.

Before comparing subscriptions, write down the work you need the system to do. A two-person consultancy handling referrals has different requirements from a growing team running inbound campaigns. Buying for a hypothetical future can leave you paying for features nobody owns.

The differences that matter

Decision HubSpot Pipedrive
Starting point Free CRM tools and paid products across a customer platform Paid sales CRM tiers, with a trial
Cost questions Which hub, seat type, feature tier and usage allowances? Which plan, how many seats and which add-ons?
A useful reason to shortlist You want customer work connected across functions Your priority is pipeline visibility and sales follow-up
A reason to pause The required capabilities may span several paid products Additional marketing or delivery needs may require other tools

What will it actually cost?

Pipedrive’s checked USD annual-billing display lists Lite at $14, Growth at $39, Premium at $59 and Ultimate at $79 per seat per month. These are annual commitments displayed as monthly equivalents, not monthly-payment prices. Add-ons and tax can change the total.

For example, three Growth seats at that displayed annual rate come to $1,404 per year before tax and extras. This is an illustrative calculation, not a quote for your account. Check that Growth contains your required workflow before treating that amount as your budget.

HubSpot offers free CRM tools, but that does not make a paid sales setup free. We are not quoting a single HubSpot paid price here because hub selection, seat types, tiers and promotions can change what you actually buy. Request or configure a quote covering the same requirements as your Pipedrive shortlist.

Compare the first-year total and renewal total separately. Include onboarding, migration help, email-marketing needs, paid integrations and time spent administering the system. A lower seat price can still produce a higher overall cost.

Run a useful pilot

Create a small set of fictional contacts and deals rather than importing your entire customer database immediately. Include a duplicate contact, an overdue follow-up and a deal with two decision makers.

Ask each salesperson to capture an enquiry, assign an owner, schedule the next action and explain the pipeline report. Check what happens when someone leaves the team. Try exporting your sample records and confirm that the fields you care about are included.

Record where people need help. A feature that exists but confuses your team may not solve the problem. This pilot is our recommended evaluation method; it is not a test we have performed for this article.

When to choose neither

If you cannot agree on sales stages or who owns follow-up, define those basics first. Another CRM will not resolve an unclear process. If you have very few active deals, a carefully maintained tracker may be enough while you establish that process.

Our decision

Choose the system that handles your real deal cycle with the fewest avoidable workarounds. Prefer HubSpot when its wider platform has a concrete role in your plan; prefer Pipedrive when the sales pipeline is the clear centre of the work. Confirm capabilities on the exact paid tier before committing.

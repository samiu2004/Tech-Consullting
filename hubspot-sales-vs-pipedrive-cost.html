HubSpot Sales Hub vs Pipedrive: First-Year Cost for a Small Team

Compare qualifying sales editions, annual commitments and setup costs before deciding which CRM fits.

The cheaper seat price may not buy the automation your team needs. A useful comparison starts with a working setup: the same team, the same required tasks and a clearly stated billing basis.

This guide compares a sales-CRM buying situation. HubSpot Marketing Hub is a different product and is not used as a substitute for Sales Hub pricing. There is no universal winner; a business that needs a broader HubSpot setup faces a different decision from one that primarily needs a sales pipeline.

Define the same job for both products

Suppose a hypothetical three-person team wants to record opportunities, connect individual email accounts, automate selected follow-ups and review its pipeline. Before selecting an edition, also specify whether it needs a shared inbox, advanced permissions or particular integrations.

Those extra requirements can change the qualifying plan. Do not assume that personal email sync means a shared team inbox is included, or that every automation condition belongs to the same edition.

Read the published price in context

Pipedrive's official pricing lists Growth at US$39 per seat per month on annual billing, equivalent to US$468 per seat per year. Its documentation places email sync and automations on Growth and higher plans. Verify the current offer and feature limits before buying.

HubSpot's product catalog lists Sales Hub Professional from US$100 per Sales Seat per month and a required US$1,500 one-time onboarding charge. Annual discounts or a particular quote can change the amount payable. These catalog figures are not a final account quote.

Three-seat example Calculation basis Illustrative first-year amount
Pipedrive Growth 3 × US$468 annual seat charge US$1,404
HubSpot Sales Professional 3 × US$100 × 12, plus US$1,500 onboarding US$5,100

The second row annualizes the catalog rate; it is not a verified annual-discount quote. Both rows exclude taxes, add-ons, migration, internal administration and other products. They illustrate how to calculate, not a claim that the editions are feature-equivalent or the final cheapest options.

Compare the constraints that can change the result

Decision Evidence to request from each vendor
Task fit Demonstration of the team's actual sales workflow
Usage limits Edition limits for the required automation and data
Setup Required onboarding, implementation and internal work
Email Personal versus shared inbox access and sending conditions
Permissions Which users can view, change or export sensitive records
Integrations Native connection, paid connector or custom work
Exit Records, history, relationships and files that can be exported

Make the same assumptions on both sides. If one system needs an additional tool, include it. If the business already pays for a feature elsewhere, do not count its value twice merely because it appears in a bundle.

Test the likely daily workflow

Create a sample enquiry, move it through the proposed stages and trigger a test follow-up. Check what happens after a reply, a missing owner or a duplicate record. A feature existing on a plan does not establish that your particular rule is supported.

Use two sample users to test visibility. Export representative records and inspect whether the information needed by your next system survives. These are proposed evaluation tasks, not results from hands-on testing performed for this guide.

Decide using a written quote and a small pilot

Pipedrive is a candidate to investigate when the requirement centers on a sales pipeline and the qualifying plan meets the team's limits. HubSpot is a candidate when the wider connected setup has real value and its total cost is acceptable. Neither description replaces a trial of the required edition.

Before signing, record the exact edition, seats, currency, billing commitment, onboarding, add-ons, renewal terms and remaining unknowns. Link each feature claim to current official documentation. If the team is already using Pipedrive and wants replacements, the alternatives guide starts with the reason for leaving rather than repeating a two-product comparison.

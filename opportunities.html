Cloud credits · AWS Activate

Give your first product a little more breathing room.

Your first customers should be the challenge—not figuring out how to cover every cloud bill. Activate can help eligible startups pay for the AWS services behind their product.

A fit for: Bootstrapped and funded product startups.

Benefits, eligibility & important limits +
What you get
Founders starts with $1,000 in credits; selected participants may qualify for up to $5,000. Portfolio offers up to $200,000 with an Activate Provider’s organisation ID.
Who can apply
Pre-Series B, founded within the last 10 years, with an AWS account on a Paid Tier Plan. Previous Activate awards affect eligibility for additional credits.
Read before applying
These are promotional service credits, not cash. A paid-plan account is required. Check eligible services, award expiry and charges that credits do not cover before using them.
Application timing
An application route is available; the programme page does not list a fixed closing date.
Location
Confirm that AWS can serve your company’s jurisdiction and that your account meets the application requirements.

Cloud credits · Google for Startups Cloud

Your MVP works. Make the next stage more affordable.

You have something people can use. Now you need to improve it without letting infrastructure costs decide every move. Start by checking which programme tier actually fits your business.

A fit for: Young product startups with a working MVP.

Benefits, eligibility & important limits +
What you get
The Start tier offers $2,000. Separate funded tiers offer up to $200,000–$350,000; those larger figures have different funding and eligibility requirements.
Who can apply
The Start tier targets startups founded within the past two years, with a working MVP, a clear business model and plans to seek venture funding.
Read before applying
Agencies, consultancies, personal blogs and several other business types are excluded. Credit history and company stage affect acceptance. Confirm expiry, covered services and post-credit billing in your offer.
Application timing
An application route is available; check the provider’s current terms when you apply.
Location
Your company must be in a region where Google Cloud is generally available through self-service purchasing. Regional and industry restrictions apply.

AI startup support · NVIDIA Inception

Building an AI product? Find support beyond another subscription.

Sometimes the next step is a better technical answer, relevant training or a useful introduction. Inception brings those resources together for qualifying AI and technology startups.

A fit for: Incorporated startups with a use case for NVIDIA technology.

Benefits, eligibility & important limits +
What you get
Technical training, developer resources, preferred pricing on selected products and partner offers. Some members may qualify for investor introductions; funding is not guaranteed.
Who can apply
An incorporated company under 10 years old, with a working website, a clear NVIDIA technology use case and at least one active founder. Revenue is not required.
Read before applying
Consulting and outsourced development firms, cloud providers, resellers and other listed business types do not qualify. No application fee, membership fee or equity requirement. Individual benefits vary.
Application timing
No application deadlines or fixed cohorts are listed by NVIDIA.
Location
Check the application route and local availability of individual partner benefits. Membership does not guarantee that every benefit is offered in every location.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

A little preparation.
A clearer decision.

Don’t move your whole product for an offer you might not receive. Check the fit first, then keep these details close.

  • Your business, clearly explained.A working website, company details and a short explanation of what you’re building.
  • The right evidence.Funding details, incorporation records or a provider referral when the programme asks for them.
  • A plan for the bill afterwards.Check expiry dates, excluded services and your monthly costs once the credits run out.

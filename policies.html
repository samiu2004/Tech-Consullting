Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

The contact form on this site collects the name, email address and message you send. Those details go to Formspree, which processes the form and delivers your enquiry to TechConsulting. We use them to respond to your enquiry or correction. Formspree handles the details you send under its own privacy policy: Formspree Privacy Policy.

There is no newsletter signup, no account system and no advertising on this site.

No analytics or tracking script loads in your browser on these pages. We do not use Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Two other services are involved in loading these pages, and nothing else. First, the text on this site uses Sora and Manrope, loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When a page loads, your browser contacts Google to fetch those font files, and Google may receive your IP address and browser details under Google’s own privacy policy. Second, the site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare serves each page you request and may process request information, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and protect the site, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Site search and the Subscription Cost Calculator both run entirely in your browser. A search query is never sent to a search service, and anything you type into the calculator stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

External links on this site lead to other services with their own privacy practices. If you follow a link, that service’s policy applies from that point on.

Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

This site collects no personal information from you. There is no contact form, no newsletter signup, no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts. We do not run Google Analytics or any other analytics service.

Two outside services are involved in loading these pages, and nothing else. First, the text on this site uses Space Grotesk and Instrument Sans, loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When a page loads, your browser contacts Google to fetch those font files, and Google may receive your IP address and browser details under Google’s own privacy policy. Second, the site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare serves each page you request and may process request information, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and protect the site, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Site search and the Subscription Cost Calculator both run entirely in your browser. A search query is never sent to a search service, and anything you type into the calculator stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

External links on this site lead to other services with their own privacy practices. If you follow a link, that service’s policy applies from that point on.

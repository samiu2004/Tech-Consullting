Proton VPN vs NordVPN: when does remote work need a VPN?

Compare Proton VPN and NordVPN for freelance work, public Wi-Fi and approved client access. Understand dedicated IPs and the limits of a VPN.

Do you actually need a VPN?

You are working from a café, opening a client dashboard and sending a proposal. A VPN can add an encrypted tunnel between your device and the VPN server. It changes the public IP address seen by websites. It does not create another computer, repair a weak internet connection or make a website trust you automatically.

HTTPS already encrypts the connection to a properly secured website. A VPN adds protection for the network path to its server, but shifts some trust to the VPN provider. It does not remove phishing, unsafe downloads or the need for multi-factor authentication.

Which websites require it?

No general rule says you need a VPN for a client website, a freelance marketplace or a social account. The requirement usually comes from the organisation’s access policy.

For a concrete example, Salesforce administrators can restrict profile logins to approved IP ranges. If your client uses that setting, ask its administrator which connection to use. It might be the client’s own corporate VPN. A random consumer VPN server will not automatically be on its approved list.

The same question applies to a private company portal, staging website or database dashboard: is access limited to a particular network? Ask before buying. A dedicated IP only helps if the client approves that address and the rest of its security requirements are met.

When a VPN helps—and when it does not

It can be useful when you regularly use shared networks, want to reduce what the local network can observe, or need an approved network route for work. A business service can also make access administration more consistent for a team.

It is not a way to acquire permission to use an account, change your legal location or guarantee that a platform will accept a login. Do not present a different country to evade account eligibility or identity checks. Keep account details accurate and follow the client’s and website’s rules.

For slow internet, test first. A VPN introduces another route and can increase latency. It cannot keep your laptop online during a connection outage. If your problem is an overloaded device or a Windows-only application, read the cloud-desktop comparison instead.

Compare the full subscription

Proton lists a free plan. Paid VPN prices can vary with location, promotion and commitment length; compare the checkout total rather than an advertised monthly equivalent. NordVPN states that subscriptions renew automatically at the applicable renewal price unless cancelled. A dedicated-IP add-on can increase the bill.

Write down the first-term total, renewal total, included devices and refund conditions. For client work, include team administration and a stable-IP requirement in that comparison. Personal and business subscriptions should not be treated as interchangeable.

Our decision

Start with Proton Free if you want to evaluate basic personal VPN use and its restrictions fit. Consider a paid Proton or NordVPN plan when you can name the feature you need. For restricted client access, let the client’s approved configuration decide the shortlist.

Before committing, test your actual meetings, uploads and permitted dashboards. Record delays and disconnections; we cannot promise performance on your connection.

How the calculation works

The calculator uses four simple formulas, shown here so you can check the result by hand.

  • Monthly equivalent of a monthly plan: the cost you typed.
  • Monthly equivalent of an annual plan: the annual cost divided by 12.
  • Monthly total: the sum of every monthly equivalent.
  • Annual total: the monthly total multiplied by 12.
  • Combined total for one year: the sum of (monthly cost times 12) for every monthly plan, plus the annual cost of every annual plan.

The annual total and the combined total for one year are two views of the same year, so they match. If they ever differ, check a row for a typing mistake.

Assumptions

  • One year has 12 months.
  • Amounts are in US dollars (USD), as typed. The calculator does not convert currencies.
  • An annual plan is spread evenly across 12 months for the monthly total, even though you pay it in one go.
  • The calculator uses the numbers you type. It does not check them against any vendor, and it does not fetch prices.

Limitations

  • The result is an estimate, not a bill. It does not include taxes, fees, per-seat charges, usage charges, discounts, free trials, or price rises.
  • An annual plan paid up front still costs the full amount at renewal, even though it is shown spread monthly here.
  • A subscription with a free plan, credits, or a first-month offer will cost less than its standard price while the offer lasts. Check the vendor page for the current terms.

Your numbers stay in your browser

Your inputs stay in the browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored on a server, or shared. There is no account and no signup, and the calculator never asks for passwords, API keys, or any sensitive details. Closing the page clears your entries.

ChatGPT Images 2.6 turns your pictures into GIFs, on every plan

Good news if you like making things with AI. OpenAI launched Images 2.6 on 4 November, and the best part is the price. It is on every subscription tier, not only the expensive plan. So you can try it without upgrading. It is available globally in ChatGPT, on desktop, mobile, and web.

By TechConsulting Editorial Published 4 November 2026 Sources checked 13 November 2026 2 min read

Good news if you like making things with AI. OpenAI launched Images 2.6 on 4 November, and the best part is the price. It is on every subscription tier, not only the expensive plan. So you can try it without upgrading. It is available globally in ChatGPT, on desktop, mobile, and web.

Three things changed that you will notice. First, speed. OpenAI says images generate 50 percent faster. Second, GIFs. You can animate a mascot, a product shot, or a simple sketch into a loop. Third, consistency. Characters, products, and brand details are meant to stay put as you edit, instead of your logo slowly melting with every revision. If you have a developer, the API version also exports SVG files, which you can drop straight onto a website or into print.

Why it matters to you: a small business can now make a simple animated product shot without paying for a higher plan. That is a free animated image for your next email or product page, made from a photo you already own.

What to do now

  • Take one product photo you own and ask for a short loop. Try this: turn this product photo into a smooth looping GIF. Keep the product, label, and colours exactly as they are. Only the background light should move.
  • Check the label at full size before you publish. AI loves to improve small print, and a misspelt ingredient is not a fun conversation with a customer.
  • Use photos you own or have the rights to, and keep the result honest. Do not present an AI image as a real photo of your product.

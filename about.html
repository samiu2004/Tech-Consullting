About TechConsulting

Technology explained with purpose.

We are building a practical resource for AI, software, and startup opportunities, with useful context for readers in Pakistan.

What you can expect

Clear explanations, source links, transparent comparisons, and actionable guides. The site is at its starting stage; this build contains an initial directory and introductory guidance, not a catalogue of completed reviews.

Editorial approach

Facts should link to original sources. Pricing and eligibility need a checked date. Opinions, testing results, and documentation summaries should be distinguishable. We will correct material errors and disclose commercial relationships.

Affiliate approach

Some future links may earn a commission. A commission will not be presented as proof that a tool is best. No affiliate tracking links have been added to this starter site.

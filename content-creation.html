Follow the publishing workflow

  1. Choose the reader’s question. Make the purpose specific before drafting.
  2. Check the evidence. Use official sources for changing facts.
  3. Add useful detail. Explain the cost, limits or practical consequence.
  4. Review before publishing. Use the full editorial checklist.

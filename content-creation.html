Content creation tools, compared before you pay.

Seven tools for making videos and voiceovers, with honest starting prices and the affiliate commission each one pays. Prices checked 13 November 2026. We have not joined these programs yet, so these are plain links to the official sites, not tracking links.

Our top pick

Top pick / Faceless video

Syllaby.io

Syllaby makes faceless videos for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It finds topics, writes the script, makes the video with AI voices and avatars, and schedules the posts for you, so one person can publish every day without filming.

Best for: creators who want to publish faceless videos every day without a camera or an editor.

Starting price: from $9 a month (Starter). Basic is $29 a month.

Affiliate commission: 30% recurring commission for life, paid through FirstPromoter.

Visit the official site ↗

Video tools with affiliate programs

Script to video

Pictory

Pictory turns a script, blog post or article into a video with stock footage, captions and AI voices.

Best for: turning written content into short videos for social media.

Starting price: from $25 a month (Starter, billed annually) or $29 a month billed monthly.

Affiliate commission: up to 50% recurring.

Visit the official site ↗

AI video generation

InVideo

InVideo makes videos from a prompt with AI models, stock footage and a full editor, for YouTube and social media.

Best for: creators who want AI video generation and a full editor in one place.

Starting price: from $20 per seat a month (Starter), or $10 per seat on annual billing. InVideo shows different prices to different visitors, so check the live page.

Affiliate commission: 50% on monthly plans, 25% on annual plans, first billing cycle only.

Visit the official site ↗

Avatar video

Synthesia

Synthesia makes presenter videos with AI avatars that speak your script in 140+ languages. No camera, no studio.

Best for: training videos, explainers and product demos.

Starting price: from $19 a month (Starter, $9 in the first month). A free Basic plan is available.

Affiliate commission: 25% of paid plan payments.

Visit the official site ↗

Avatar video

HeyGen

HeyGen makes avatar videos and translates your videos into 175+ languages with your own cloned voice and matching lip sync.

Best for: talking head videos and reaching viewers in other languages.

Starting price: from $29 a month (Creator), or $24 a month on annual billing.

Affiliate commission: Tiered 20% / 25% / 30% for 6 months.

Visit the official site ↗

Short clips

OpusClip

OpusClip cuts long videos and podcasts into short captioned clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and scores each clip for virality.

Best for: turning one long video into a week of short clips.

Starting price: from $15 a month (Starter). A free plan is available.

Affiliate commission: 25% recurring for 12 months.

Visit the official site ↗

AI voice

ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs makes lifelike AI voices for narration, voice cloning, dubbing, music and sound effects.

Best for: voiceovers for faceless videos, podcasts and audiobooks.

Starting price: from $6 a month (Starter). We could not verify this on the official page on 13 November 2026, so please check the live page. A free plan is available.

Affiliate commission: not yet verified against the official programme page.

Visit the official site ↗

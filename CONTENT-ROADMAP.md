## Topic selection

Choose topics that answer a concrete reader question. Prioritise usefulness, original-source evidence, relevance to the audience, and the ability to keep changing details up to date. Affiliate availability should not decide the recommendation.

Example topic briefs, not current claims:
- Which AI assistant suits a beginner's writing workflow?
- What should a developer compare before buying an AI coding subscription?
- What is the total first-term and renewal cost of a VPS?
- Does a startup offer provide cash, credits, or a discount, and are Pakistani applicants eligible?

Do not publish a “cheapest” claim without a defined comparison set, billing period, checked prices, and relevant limitations.

## Article skeleton

1. Title that names the reader's question.
2. Author, published date, and updated date.
3. Short answer and intended audience.
4. Evidence: original sources, tested method, or clearly identified documentation research.
5. Main explanation, comparison, or numbered walkthrough.
6. Cost, limits, regional access, and prerequisites where relevant.
7. Alternatives and situations where the product is unsuitable.
8. Official next-step links; disclose any affiliate links beside the relevant recommendation.
9. Source list and correction information.

Separate article pages will add to the current ten-page count. Until then, publish entries inside the relevant category page. Never reuse a demo subscription confirmation or fabricated review score.

## Visual system

Shared dark palette with blue, teal, and purple accents; particle network; floating orbs; circular rings; sliding headings; scroll reveals; CTA sheen and hover feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep article paragraphs readable and motion away from the main reading column.
